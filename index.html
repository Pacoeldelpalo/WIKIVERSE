SPHEREx

SPHEREx is a NASA space telescope that observes the entire sky in near-infrared. It measures each region in 102 colors to study the physics of cosmic inflation, the history of galaxies, and water ice and other molecules where stars form.

It launched in March 2025, began mapping in May, and completed its first full-sky map in December 2025. Each day it observes a different strip of sky, with about 3,600 images, and revisits the entire sky every six months. Its primary mission plans four full-sky maps over two years.

Wikiverse

Wikiverse is a public web tool for space analysis. It brings sky images together with their data (coordinates, date, channel, and detector) and lets people explore them by coordinates or in the six-part image-of-the-day views.

About us

We believe looking at the universe should be as easy as browsing a library. We built Wikiverse so anyone can compare how the same patch of sky changes over time, without a technical background.
